📝 Student Test Instructions

Step 1: Generate Your Login Details

1️⃣ Go to the Registration Page.

2️⃣ Enter your Full Name in the form.

3️⃣ Click Register and wait.

4️⃣ Your Username and Password will appear in a popup.

5️⃣ Write them down or take a screenshot. Do not share!

Step 2: Log In

1️⃣ Go to the Login Page.

2️⃣ Enter the Username and Password you generated.

3️⃣ Click Login.

4️⃣ You’ll be taken to the Test Page.

💡 Important Notes:
  • Complete the test in one sitting.
  • Ensure your internet connection is stable.
  • Login works only once unless permission is given to retry.
  • Your Camera must be switched on while taking the test
