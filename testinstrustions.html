IT Student Test Instructions

Welcome to the IT Test Portal! Please read these instructions carefully before starting your test.

Test Details

Duration: 50 minutes – Timer starts once you click “Start Test”.
  • Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) – Choose the correct option.
  • Short Answer Questions – Type your answer in the space provided.

During the Test

  • You cannot pause or restart once the test begins.
  • Navigate between questions using the Next and Previous buttons.
  • The system will auto-submit when time runs out.
  • The system will auto-submit when your face is not detected.
  • You may click Submit Test at any time before the timer ends.

After the Test

  • Log out once finished.
Important Rules:
  1. Do not refresh or close the browser during the test.
  2. Cheating will lead to disqualification.
  3. Ensure a stable internet connection before starting.
  4. You can only attempt the quiz once, multiple submissions will be rejected
